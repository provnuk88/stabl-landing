How a market works

A price is a sharper answer
than an opinion.

  1. 01

    A question is posted

    Will Frankel’s Legacy win the Gold Cup? Every market resolves to yes or no, against a named public source, and to nothing else.

  2. 02

    The price is the probability

    Contracts trade between 1¢ and 99¢. At 72¢ the market is saying it believes there is a 72% chance. Buy that side, sell it, or write the other.

  3. 03

    The result settles it

    When the race is run, winning contracts pay $1 and losing contracts pay nothing. No bookmaker sets the line. The market does, and it moves all week.
